Protocol verification case study

WETH9 Large Deposits: Event-Level Verification

A fixed-window Ethereum investigation that separates function-selector evidence from verified protocol execution. The canonical row-level dataset contains 135 successful native-ETH calls of at least 1,000 ETH to WETH9 from 56 sender addresses.

135 / 135 fully verified
128 + 7 selector-only + trailing
5 / 1 / 1 observed trailing patterns

The evidence establishes Deposit-event presence, exact amount-sum equality, and sender-to-Deposit-destination consistency. It does not attribute semantic meaning, wallet software, entity ownership, shared infrastructure, or economic intent to the trailing bytes.
